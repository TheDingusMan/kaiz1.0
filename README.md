# Kaiz(1.)0

A very super hard version of deltraune (hard mode), which is a harder version of deltraune, which is based off of DELTARUNE and inspired by Sunderfail, which is based off of UNDERTALE.

## Controls

Arrow keys = Move, move menu

Z / Enter = Confirm, read text

X / Shift = Exit, skip text

C / Control = Auto skip and read text

` = Reset game

## Gameplay
<details>
  <summary>SPOILERS, READ AT YOUR OWN RISK!</summary>

  
  1.0 has three phases and has 438426 HP in phases 1 and 3, but a random amount of around over 100 HP for phase 2. Unless TIRED, in phases 1 and 3 if you try to attack him normally, he will deflect it with his Hammer and it will deal 0 damage. If you deal damage other than that, he will still try to block it with the Hammer, making it deal half of its original damage. The only way to stop this is to make him TIRED by leaving him with under 109606 HP (1/4 of his max HP), which makes him spareable with 100% TP and also breaks his Hammer, making him unable to deflect or block attacks.
   
</details>
